# Heat v2 catalog - every measured spot, documented (2026-09-21)

This folder is the permanent record of heat v2 band 1. Nothing here is a working copy: do not edit, move or
delete; add new bands as new files. Method and history: `main/theory/heatmap/docs\HEATMAP.txt` (section "The pattern,
in equations" explains what heat is and why it has the structure it has).

## What was measured

Every (column, row) pair in the whole 30M world (chunks -1,874,999..+1,874,999 on both axes) whose SEPARABLE
PREDICTION is >= 12 ("band 1", 109,127,698 pairs), each measured directly with the class estimator
(`hotspot_scan --heat-class`, 16,384 classes ~ 300k plain seeds, brute-force self-check on every run). Reads >= 6.5
were kept: 109,040,528 spots, 0 duplicates, 0 malformed rows. Run: 2026-09-21 16:12-17:53, laptop-class GPU (6 GB), 17,978 pairs/s.

SUPERSEDED 2026-09-30 (see docs\HEATMAP.txt "CATALOG COVERAGE"): band 1 holds ~88% of the 22+ spots, not all of
them - 1M-spot samples of pred 10-12 and 8-10 measured 22+ rates of 7.5e-5 and 2.0e-6, about 46,000 spots below the
cutoff. The old claim below rested on 20,000 samples a band, too few for a 7.5e-5 rate.
Band 1 contains ALL spots with true heat 22+ (0 of 400k validation spots predicted below 10 reached 22) and ~90% of
20+. It does NOT contain every 13+ spot: the validation estimates ~245M spots at true 13+ world-wide, about half of
them predicted below 12 (bands 10-12, 8-10, ... not yet run; plans for 10-12 are in `inputs/plan/`).

Measured heat carries read noise (sd ~11.5% at 12, ~8% at 22-40), so the tier counts are of READS: e.g. 331,595
reads >= 22 against ~150k spots of TRUE heat 22+ - many 20-22 spots read high. Rank by heat, but treat +-2 as a tie.

Columns (all files):
- `rank` - position in the whole catalog by measured heat
- `cx, cz` - chunk coordinates of the spot (disc centre); `x_block, z_block` = 16 cx, 16 cz (the hunting lists' convention)
- `heat40` - measured heat (1 = a random place); `sd` - standard error of this read; `classes` - 16,384
- `pred` - separable prediction F(cx) G(cz) / 341.277 that put the pair in the band
- `line`, `line_offset` - nearest Z line n and cz - (48.5 + 96.875 n)
- `v1_region` - `v1` (the exact spot was in the old 3M survey), `v1_other_row` (old column and old line, a different
  row), `A` (old top-10k column, line outside the old top 300), `B` (old column rank 10k-70k, old top-300 line),
  `new` (anything else)
- `near_border` - 1 if within 8 chunks of the world border (heat computed as if the world continued; check in game)
- `screen_best_chunks` - best chunk count from any universe/floor screen of this spot (blank = never screened)
- `search_best_cq` - best cq from an exact search of this spot (blank = never exact-searched)
