# Second pass - findings (tier 1 run from 2026-09-22)

The second pass (`heat\second_pass.ps1`) re-screens spots the fast screen put at 68-73 with the stage-2 screen
(probe 8, exhaust 128, top 1%), richest first by first-screen pairs68, and exact-searches every one reaching 74+.
Separate from the main search. Analysis script: `main\work\second_pass_study\section1.py`.

Second-pass facts worth keeping:
- **The first screen can be far off**: exact minus first-screen count ran 1-8 for the hits (median ~4). One spot
  screened at 68 is really 76. The earlier study (random sample, 0 risers of 999 at 68) under-counted: 6 of the 56
  hits came from 68, 7 from 69, 11 from 70, 13 from 71, 12 from 72, 7 from 73.
- **Stage 2 was exact on every hit**: exact best count = stage-2 count for all 56 (the stage-2 screen found the
  true best each time), so stage 2 + exact search of its 74+ loses nothing it sees.
- **pairs68 orders the work well but cannot filter**: first-screen pairs68 of the hits median 27 vs 5 for all
  68-73 spots, but one hit had 1. Ordered by it, 44 of the 56 hits came in the first 5,000 of 17,336 spots.
- Below 68 pairs68 is 0 by definition (it counts layouts at 68+), so it cannot steer a lower floor. 66-67 in this
  section: 10,738 spots (~5.7 min of stage 2); fam66 does not separate either (hits min 3, 5,942 of them pass).
- Main search: the screen was exact at 202 of 232 candidates, 1-3 under at the other 30.
- Only 3 farms were filed in section 1: after the restart most farms found were already in main\records, so the
  duplicate check (correctly) did not file them again. "Best new farm" in the log is therefore not the section's best.

## Floor test 65-67 (section 1, 2026-09-22) - keep the floor at 68

12,166 section-1 spots screened 65-67 (8 min): stage 2 raised 4,415 of them, but only **3 reached 74, all first
screened 67, all exactly 74 after the exact search** (cq 65.4-66.2 - weak, not record-capable); 66 and 65 gave none.
At 68 the same section gave 6 hits including 75+. The drop below 68 is real (in tier 1's hottest section), so the
floor stays 68 - 67 would add ~3.5 min a section for no record-grade spots.

## Optimization review 2026-09-30 (before section 3) - what is closed

Section time: screen 600 s (23%), exact search + finish 990 s (37%), second pass 1,056 s (40%).
- **AFK position cut in the exact search: REJECTED.** Over 507 searched spots the winning layout (by -39 blocks) sits
  at the screen's best position only 72% of the time, within +-3 chunks 96%. Any cut loses farms; 10 positions stay.
  (`main\work\coverage\positions.py`. Winners: (0,0) 230, (+3,0) 73, (-3,0) 53, (0,+3) 40, (+-6,0) 45.)
- **Heat at 1,024 classes as a first stage: ~20% on band runs, adopt for the queue.** 1M spots in 8 s (vs 53 s at
  16,384); keep read >= 8 retains all 75 known 22+ of the 10-12 band sample but that is 67% of spots, so band 10-12
  goes ~8 h -> ~6.5 h. Threshold 8 has thin margin (lowest true-22+ read was 8.67) - recheck before a final run.
- **Lower chunk thresholds as a cheap filter: FAILED.** T=36/1,024, T=34/512, T=32/256 all cost 17 s per 1M and would
  keep 84-99.6% of spots to retain the 75 hits - the heat40 tail is what selects 22+.
- **Bit-sliced (32 consecutive classes per thread) heat estimator: CLOSED.** It needs many classes of one family, and
  family correlation is what already killed heatFamKernel; the samples add little information.
- Open, ~15% of the second pass: fuse the two screen tiers in phase_prepass so a 68-73 spot's setup and its first
  0.25% family probes are reused instead of recomputed (~150 s a section, needs to reproduce today's numbers exactly).
