## D. Theory
1. **Why a family is rich** — phases come from B(0) of (seed + P) ^ C; without the xor, phase
   differences between chunks would not depend on the seed at all. The xor's carry pattern (C >> 20
   = 0x5E43, 8 of 15 class bits flipped) decides which (family, class) pairs align. A structural rule
   could skip even the exact enumeration.
2. **Position quality** — possible-count statistics vs actual big layouts across many spots (not
   just around one), now that the data is logged.
3. **Heat model interaction** — the product model F(X) x G(Z) fits at chi2 1.93 per measurement: a
   real X x Z interaction exists; small, unexplained.
4. **Holes in the middle** — your earlier idea that the scoring favours layouts with holes near the
   AFK spot; theorised, not changed (you preferred to check manually).

### Judgment on the full predictive model (2026-09-20, maintainer's read - opinion, not measurement)

- **A much stronger prior: probably reachable (~70% confidence).** geo_rank's -0.29 comes from ONE hand-picked
  statistic fitted on 1,724 biased labels. More features plus ~50,000 screen-labelled spots should do clearly better.
- **Naming record seeds with no search: probably not possible.** Not because it is hard, but because of the SHAPE of
  today's negatives. Exploitable structure usually leaks somewhere - locked pairs, a skewed subset, a lattice. Four
  places were checked (class-independent pairs, H structure of the aligned set, H clustering of the possible set,
  possible count) and three returned chance while the +8.5-count lift refused to decompose into anything. That is the
  pattern of a quantity you can only get by trying: the seed mixing is a hash, and the target is a maximum over 2^35
  tries of a hash-like function - like asking for the best chess move without looking ahead.
- **A ceiling argument that does not depend on that hunch:** spot ceilings span only ~65-78. A maximum over a huge
  pool concentrates into a narrow band like that by itself, so much of the spread may be extreme-value behaviour that
  no map statistic can reach. Expect a good geometry model to say "this spot is promising", not "this spot holds a 77"
  - which is all it needs to do, now that the screen and the exact search are fast.
- **What would change this:** if the fitted prior from timeline item 1 comes out clearly stronger than -0.5, geometry
  carries more than assumed here and naming seeds directly deserves another look.

### Why the heat survey cannot stay the map (coverage, measured 2026-09-20)

`main\theory\heat\spot_measured.csv` is a **300 x 10,000 grid**: 300 distinct Z rows and 10,000 X values = 3,000,000
spots. Mean gap between used Z rows: **12,495 chunks**; X spacing ~375 chunks, while a spot spans about 9. The world
holds roughly **3 x 10^11 distinct spots** (same-farm rule: +-4 chunks X, +-2 rows Z), so the survey evaluated about
**one spot in 100,000** - and the 183,536-spot universe is the top 6% of that sample, ranked by heat, whose
correlation with a spot's exact best is only 0.10-0.23.
So every record so far comes from a 0.001% sample ordered by a weak statistic. The best farm in the world is almost
certainly at a spot that was never evaluated at all - not ranked low, never looked at.
Heat cannot be extended: measuring it costs 2^18 * 222 per spot, which is why the survey is a grid and not a sweep.
The geometry prior costs ~2,000 operations and needs no seed, so it CAN be: prior over never-surveyed spots (GPU) ->
probe screen on the best of them (0.24 s) -> exact search -> in-game. That is what replaces heat.

### Sweep arithmetic (2026-09-20, after the groundwork above)

Geometry is the ONLY statistic cheap enough to run everywhere (2e3 ops; random seed draws fail, docs\PREDICTION.txt 9a), so
a world sweep looks like: geo prior over the spot lattice -> probe screen the top -> exact search the best of those.
The screen is the bottleneck, not the prior: at 0.24 s/spot, 100,000 screened spots = ~7 h of GPU. With the fitted
prior's top decile holding a 74+ at 9.3% against 4.1%, 100,000 geometry-selected NEVER-SURVEYED spots is a far bigger
hunting ground than the 167,000 left in the heat-20 universe, and it costs the same GPU hours.
Engineering left: port geo_rank to GPU (it sorts 222 values per position x 15 positions), and decide the spot lattice
to sweep (same-farm rule: +-4 chunks X, +-2 rows Z).
